Questions

Questions we hear every day.

Will I look fake?

No. We go slowly and start with less. The goal is you, looking rested and fresh, not changed.

Who will do my treatment?

A licensed doctor. You'll meet them at your consultation and can ask anything.

Does it hurt?

Most treatments feel like a quick pinch or light pressure. We'll tell you what you'll feel before we start, and we can pause any time.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the treatment and how much you need. Message us for current rates. You'll get a clear price at your consultation before anything is done.

Can I go back to work after?

For many treatments, yes. Some, like threads or fillers, can leave light swelling or bruising for a few days. The doctor will tell you what to expect.

Do I need to book ahead?

It's best to, so the doctor has time for you. Message us on Facebook and pick a day, Monday to Saturday.
